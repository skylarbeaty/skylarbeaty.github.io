    About Me

    Hi :3 Most people call me Liz. I’ve been making games for about as long as I can remember. I started programming when I was around 12 with Game Maker and GML. Over the years I’ve used a bunch of tools and languages, and I have been using Unity for over ten years.

    I really enjoy gameplay programming, especially making prototypes that iterate on play testing feedback. I’m a huge fan of any game that feels great to play, I try to make games that are juicy and tactile. I love working with procedural generation and graphics programming.

    When I’m not programming I tend to play a lot of games. I love playing D&D and other TTRPGs. I regularly host board gaming nights. I love going out to Commander nights at my local game store. I also maintain an Ubuntu server that runs game servers, photo backups, notetaking cloud sync, and a few other tasks.
